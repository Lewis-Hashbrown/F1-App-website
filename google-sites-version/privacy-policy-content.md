## 1. Introduction

Welcome to F1 Widget 2025 ("we," "our," or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application F1 Widget 2025 (the "App"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring your data is handled responsibly.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information We DO NOT Collect

F1 Widget 2025 is designed with privacy in mind. We do not collect any of the following:

- ❌ Personal identification information (name, email address, phone number)
- ❌ Location data or GPS coordinates  
- ❌ Device identifiers or advertising IDs
- ❌ Usage analytics or tracking data
- ❌ Contacts, photos, or other personal files
- ❌ Search history or browsing data

### 2.2 Information We May Collect

The App may only collect minimal technical information necessary for basic functionality:

- ✅ **Timezone Information:** To display Formula 1 session times in your local timezone
- ✅ **App Preferences:** Settings you configure within the app (stored locally on your device)
- ✅ **Crash Reports:** If the app crashes, basic technical information may be collected to improve stability (no personal data included)

---

## 3. How We Use Information

The limited information we may collect is used solely for:

- Displaying Formula 1 session times in your correct timezone
- Maintaining your app preferences and widget settings
- Improving app stability and performance
- Providing customer support when requested

---

## 4. Data Storage and Security

### 4.1 Local Storage
All app preferences and settings are stored locally on your device. We do not maintain any servers or databases containing your personal information.

### 4.2 External Data Sources
F1 Widget 2025 retrieves Formula 1 schedule information from official Formula 1 sources. This data fetching does not involve sharing any of your personal information.

### 4.3 Security Measures
- No personal data transmission to external servers
- Local data encryption for app preferences
- Secure connections when fetching F1 schedule data
- Regular security audits of the app code

---

## 5. Third-Party Services

### 5.1 Formula 1 Data Sources
The app fetches race schedule information from official Formula 1 APIs and websites. These requests do not include any personal information about you.

### 5.2 Google Play Services
As an Android app distributed through Google Play Store, the app may use basic Google Play Services for app updates and security. Please refer to Google's Privacy Policy for information about their data practices.

### 5.3 No Analytics or Advertising
F1 Widget 2025 does not use any third-party analytics services, advertising networks, or tracking technologies.

## 6. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your information to third parties. Since we collect minimal to no personal data, there is essentially nothing to share. We may only disclose information if required by law or to protect our rights, property, or safety.

F1 Widget 2025 is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Since our app collects minimal data overall, it is safe for children to use.
