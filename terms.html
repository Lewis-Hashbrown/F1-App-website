Trial and purchase terms

Last updated 17 September 2026. These terms cover Theme Packs in the F1 Schedule Widgets app. The schedule, widgets and reminders are free.

Free trial

  • The trial unlocks all 33 team and driver themes for 14 days.
  • You start it by signing in with a Google account. The 14 days start when you sign in.
  • Each Google account can use the trial once.
  • The trial ends by itself. There is no subscription, nothing to cancel and no charge.
  • When it ends, any theme you have not bought goes back to the Standard look. Themes you bought stay active.
  • How we handle your sign-in details is explained in the privacy policy.

Buying themes

  • You can buy a single team or driver theme, or all 33 themes as the 2026 collection.
  • Each purchase is a one-time payment through Google Play. It is not a subscription.
  • Every theme works on both widget sizes.
  • You do not need to sign in to the app to buy themes. Your purchases belong to your Google Play account.
  • Google Play shows the final price in your currency before you pay.

Upgrading to the full collection

If you already own single themes, the collection costs less. Up to 2 single themes you bought are taken off the collection price.

The 2026 season

Theme Packs are made for the 2026 season. You keep the themes you buy after the season ends. The 2026 collection does not include themes for future seasons. If we release themes for a future season, 2026 collection owners will be offered them at a discount.

Getting your themes on a new phone

Install the app, sign in to Google Play with the same account, open Theme Packs and tap Restore purchases.

Refunds

Google Play processes all payments, so refunds follow Google Play's refund policy. If a theme you bought doesn't work, email us and we will help. Nothing in these terms affects your legal rights as a consumer.

Changes

If Formula 1 teams or drivers change during the season, we may update theme artwork. We will not remove a theme you have bought.
