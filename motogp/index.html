The whole race weekend, on your home screen.

An Android widget that shows every session of the next MotoGP Grand Prix, in your own time zone. Free Practice, Practice, Qualifying, the Sprint, Warm Up and the Race, all at a glance.

Coming soon to Google Play Free. Android 7 and newer.

Five sizes, each with its own design

Pick the shape that fits your home screen. Each size has its own layout, not a stretched copy of the others. Prefer a simpler look? Classic 2x2 and Classic 3x2 are there too.

What it does

All 8 sessions

The widget shows the next session big, then a row with the sessions after it, with the Race always at the end. Hide the sessions you don't care about and the row makes room for the rest.

Your time zone

Session times are converted for you, wherever you are. Daylight saving is handled too.

Reminders you choose

Get a notification before the sessions you care about. Each session has its own switch, and you pick how long before. Reminders keep working after your phone restarts.

Live race calendar

Session times update by themselves when the schedule changes. Cancelled races are skipped, so the widget always shows the next race that is really happening.

Works offline

The calendar is saved inside the app, so times and reminders work with no signal. When you are online, the app checks once a day for changes.

Track maps

A map of the circuit for all 22 rounds of 2026, right on the widget.

Theme Packs for all 11 teams and 22 riders

Give your widget your team's colours or your rider's number. Every theme works on all five widget sizes. The Standard look stays free.

What the app does with your data

The short version. The privacy policy has the detail.

No ads or tracking

There are no adverts and no analytics. The widget needs no account. The app downloads the public race calendar once a day and sends nothing about you.

Settings stay on your phone

Your reminder choices and theme are stored on your device.

The free trial uses Google sign-in

To stop repeat trials, we keep an account ID and the date your trial started. Your email is held by Google's Firebase sign-in service. You can ask us to delete it.

Google Play handles payments

We never see your card details. The app asks Google Play which themes you own.
